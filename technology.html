Security technology

Technology that works as hard as you do.

Plain-language explanations of the systems we install and support.

CCTV Systems

Cameras record activity across your premises so events can be reviewed with confidence. A good system is planned around coverage, lighting and storage, not just the number of cameras.

Image: surveillance-control-room.jpg

IP Cameras

Network cameras send high-quality video over your existing network, are easy to expand, and can be viewed on approved devices.

Image: ip-camera.jpg

Remote Monitoring

Authorised people can check live views and receive alerts from anywhere, so a site is never out of sight.

Image: remote-monitoring.jpg

Access Control

Readers and credentials manage who may enter each area, with a clear record of every entry.

Image: access-control.jpg

Alarm Systems

Sensors detect unexpected entry or emergencies and trigger alerts straight away.

Image: alarm-system.jpg

Smart Security Integration

Cameras, alarms and access control can work as one platform, so an alert in one place can inform what happens in another.

Image: smart-integration.jpg

System Maintenance

Security only works when it is working. Regular checks, updates and repairs keep systems dependable.

Image: maintenance.jpg
