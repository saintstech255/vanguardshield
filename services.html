CCTV Installation and Surveillance Systems

We design, supply and install surveillance systems with clear coverage planning, tidy cabling and straightforward operation. Every layout starts with a site walk-through and an honest view of what each camera needs to see.

  • Site survey and coverage planning
  • Indoor, outdoor and perimeter camera placement
  • Recording, storage and playback set-up
  • Handover and user guidance

VIP and Executive Protection

  • Advance planning and route awareness
  • Discreet close protection
  • Coordination with venues and drivers
  • Clear communication with the principal's team

Manned Guarding and Security Personnel

A visible, professional guard presence deters problems and reassures people. Personnel are briefed on the site, its procedures and what is expected of them at every post.

  • Static and gate posts
  • Reception and access screening
  • Patrol routines and incident logging
  • Supervision and reporting to the client

Event Security Management

Good event security is mostly invisible. We plan entry, flow, restricted areas and response procedures with the organiser so guests feel welcome and secure.

  • Pre-event risk review
  • Guest entry and accreditation support
  • Crowd flow and area control
  • On-site coordination with organisers

Alarm Systems and Intrusion Detection

We specify and install intrusion detection suited to the building and how it is used, from sensors and panels to sirens and notifications.

  • Door, window and motion detection
  • Panic and emergency triggers
  • Alert notifications to nominated contacts
  • Testing and user training

Access Control Systems

Access control replaces loose keys with managed permissions. Cards, codes or other credentials can be issued, limited and withdrawn quickly as staff and visitors change.

  • Card, PIN or biometric readers
  • Zone and time-based permissions
  • Visitor management options
  • Entry logs for review
